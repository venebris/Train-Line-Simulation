How the line works

Track and signals. Local trains stay on the light grey main line and stop at every station. Express trains move onto a lower passing loop at stations they skip. The main line and loop have separate signal occupancy, so an express can use the loop in a section while a local uses the main line in that same section. Near a loop exit, the train closer to the next signal section goes first; if they are equally close, the express has priority. The local waits at its station when yielding, and the express continues to the loop exit.

Services. Local trains call at every station. Express trains always call at the first and final stations, and stop at the intermediate stations listed in the settings. They use the lower track to bypass all other intermediate stations. The first express is scheduled after the configured delay. The time between trains is the minimum interval between scheduled departures; if the first signal section is occupied, the next departure stays queued off the map until the section clears.

Movement. Acceleration / deceleration and top speed control how quickly trains travel. Dwell time is how long a train waits at a stop. Simulation speed controls how quickly the animation advances in real time; at 1x, it advances about 30 simulated seconds per real second.

Controls. Change settings, then press R to restart with them. Press Space to pause or resume. ← and → pause and move backward or forward by five simulation steps.
